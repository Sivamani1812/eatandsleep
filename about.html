We Help Families Sleep Again

Welcome

If you're reading this at 2 AM with a crying baby in your arms, wondering what you're doing wrong — stop right there. You're not doing anything wrong. Babies don't come with instruction manuals, and sleep is one of the hardest parts to figure out.

We're certified baby sleep consultants who specialize in helping families with newborns and toddlers find rest without losing their minds in the process. Whether you're dealing with sleep regression, night wakings, or bedtime battles, we provide sleep training support that respects your parenting style. Founded by K. Sree Banu, we've worked with exhausted parents across the globe, and we understand what you're going through because we've been there too.

Our story Eat Right Sleep Right

started with one simple belief: every family deserves peaceful nights and the support to get there.
K. Sree Banu founded this practice after seeing too many parents struggle alone — reading conflicting advice online, trying sleep methods that didn't fit their family, feeling guilty for being tired. As a certified baby sleep consultant with training in infant sleep patterns and child development, she knew there had to be a better way. One that respects both the baby's needs and the parents' sanity.

We don't believe in one-size-fits-all solutions or rigid sleep schedules. We don't push methods that feel wrong to you. And we definitely don't judge you for whatever you've tried before that didn't work.

What we do believe in: gentle guidance, evidence-based strategies, and honest conversations about what's realistic for your family.

What Makes Us Different

Evidence-Based, Not Trendy

We're not following the latest parenting fad. Our approach as baby sleep specialists is rooted in actual sleep science — understanding infant development, sleep cycles, circadian rhythms, and age-appropriate sleep expectations. We use methods that have been studied and proven to work, including techniques from pediatric sleep research and behavioral sleep medicine. We combine this knowledge with practical experience from working with real families, not just textbook cases.

Certified Expertise You Can Trust

K. Sree Banu is a certified baby sleep consultant with specialized training in newborn care, toddler sleep development, and gentle sleep coaching methods. We understand sleep associations, wake windows, nap transitions, and how to troubleshoot common sleep challenges like early morning waking and bedtime resistance. We stay current with research, understand developmental milestones, and know how to adjust our approach based on your child's unique temperament and your family's situation. This isn't guesswork. It's trained, professional pediatric sleep guidance.

Personalized for Your Family

Your baby isn't like every other baby. Your schedule isn't like every other schedule. Your comfort level with sleep training methods might be completely different from your best friend's. We get that. Every consultation starts with understanding your specific situation — your baby's age and temperament, current sleep habits and wake times, what you've already tried, and what feels right to you as a parent. Then we build a customized sleep plan around that. Not the other way around.

Gentle Methods That Feel Right

We're often asked: "Do you teach cry-it-out methods?" Here's our answer: We offer responsive, gentle sleep training approaches that help your baby learn independent sleep skills without leaving them alone to cry. We work within your comfort zone using gradual techniques that build confidence for both baby and parents. If a method doesn't feel right to you, we don't use it. Period. Sleep coaching doesn't have to mean abandoning your instincts or your baby's attachment needs. We help you find the balance between supporting your baby and teaching them healthy sleep habits they need.

Whole-Family Support

Sleep doesn't exist in a vacuum. It's connected to feeding schedules, nap routines, bedtime routines, daily rhythms, stress levels, and a dozen other things happening in your household. As an infant sleep expert team, we look at the complete picture — not just bedtime. We talk about nap schedules, feeding patterns (breastfeeding, bottle feeding, solid foods), your own rest, family schedules, sleep environment, and how everything fits together. Because when one piece is off, it affects everything else.

Real Support, Not Just a PDF

You won't get a generic sleep plan emailed to you with a "good luck" attached. We work with you one-on-one through video consultations, checking in as you implement sleep strategies, troubleshooting when things don't go as planned, and adjusting the approach as your baby grows and developmental changes happen. This is ongoing sleep coaching support. We're in it with you until your family is sleeping better.

Our Philosophy

We believe:

Sleep is a learned skill, not a talent.

Some babies pick it up easily. Others need more guidance and practice with self-soothing. Neither is wrong — they're just different.

Parents know their babies best.

We're here to guide and support, not to override your parental instincts. If something doesn't feel right, we figure out another way.

There's no perfect parent.

You're going to have rough nights and sleep setbacks even after things improve. That's normal child development. We help you build foundations that bounce back from regressions.

Gentle doesn't mean slow.

Responsive sleep methods and gradual approaches can still be effective. You don't have to choose between being kind to your baby and getting results.

Every family is different.

What works for one might not work for another. We customize everything to fit your situation, your values, and your child.

You deserve sleep too.

Taking care of yourself isn't selfish — it makes you a better parent. We help the whole family rest, not just the baby.

FAQ

The safest sleep position for a one-month-old is on the back. This reduces the risk of Sudden Infant Death Syndrome (SIDS) and ensures proper breathing. Use a firm mattress, fitted sheet, and avoid pillows or loose blankets in the crib.

We use gentle, science-backed strategies tailored to each child. These include consistent bedtime routines, age-appropriate nap schedules, soothing techniques like swaddling or white noise, and gradual self-soothing methods. Every family’s needs are unique, so we create customized sleep plans.

Experts suggest delaying bed-sharing until after 12 months to reduce safety risks. However, room-sharing is recommended during the first year, with the baby sleeping in a crib or bassinet close to the parents. This balances safety with parental comfort.
